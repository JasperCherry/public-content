PRIVACY POLICY FOR ROADMEN
Effective Date: 15 October 2026

1. INTRODUCTION
   Roadmen ("the App", Android package com.jaspercherry.roadmen) is a
   lane-battle game. Your privacy is important, and this Privacy Policy
   explains how information is handled when you use the App.

2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT
   The developer does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

Specifically:

* No account registration is required
* No names, emails, or contact details are collected
* No location data is collected
* No analytics or tracking by the developer
* No in-app purchases

The only data that leaves your device is handled by Google AdMob for
advertising (see sections 6 and 7).

3. LOCAL DATA (GAME PROGRESS ONLY)
   The App stores your game progress locally on your device, such as:

* Cities beaten and stars earned
* Units recruited and their levels
* Chicken wings (in-game currency) and your chosen squad

This data:

* Never leaves your device
* Is not transmitted to any servers
* Is stored only so you can continue your game

4. DATA RETENTION AND DELETION
   Since the developer does not collect or store personal data externally:

* No personal data is retained by the developer
* All game progress exists only locally on your device

You can delete all stored data at any time by:

* Clearing the app storage via your device settings, or
* Uninstalling the App

Uninstalling the App will permanently delete all game progress.

5. DEVICE PERMISSIONS
   The App does not request any runtime permissions. It uses:

* Internet / network state:
  Used only to load ads

* Advertising ID:
  Used by Google AdMob to serve and measure ads

The App does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, location,
photos or files.

6. ADVERTISING
   The App shows rewarded ads from Google AdMob, only when you choose
   to watch one in exchange for in-game chicken wings. The App never
   shows ads during gameplay.

   To serve and measure ads, Google may collect your device's
   advertising ID, IP address, general device information and how you
   interact with ads. Google's use of this data is described in:

* How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services:
  https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
* Google Privacy Policy:
  https://policies.google.com/privacy

   If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or
   Switzerland, the App asks for your consent before any ads are
   requested. You can change your choice at any time with "Privacy
   options" at the bottom of the main menu. You can also reset or delete
   your advertising ID in your device's Android settings.

7. DATA SHARING AND THIRD-PARTY SERVICES
   The developer does not share any data with third parties. The only
   third-party service in the App is Google AdMob (Google LLC), which
   processes the advertising data described in section 6 under its own
   privacy policy.

   The App does not use:

* Analytics tools
* Crash reporting or tracking services
* Any server owned by the developer

8. DATA SECURITY
   Game progress stays on your device. Data sent to Google AdMob is
   encrypted in transit.

9. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY
   The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly
   collect any personal data from them.

10. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY
    This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Updates will be
    posted with a revised Effective Date.
