PRIVACY POLICY FOR MOTION ALARM
Effective Date: 15 October 2026

1. INTRODUCTION
   Motion Alarm ("the App", Android package com.jaspercherry.motionalarm)
   provides motion-based alarm functionality. Your privacy is important,
   and this Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you
   use the App.

2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT
   The developer does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

Specifically:

* No account registration is required
* No names, emails, or contact details are collected
* No location data is collected
* No analytics or tracking by the developer

The only data that leaves your device goes to Google: for ads (Google
AdMob, see section 7) and for the optional Remove ads purchase (Google
Play, see section 8).

3. LOCAL DATA (SETTINGS AND ALARM LOG)
   The App stores the following locally on your device:

* Settings, such as sensitivity, timers and light pattern
* The alarm log: when you armed and disarmed the alarm, when it was
  triggered and how strong the movement was

This data:

* Never leaves your device
* Is not transmitted to any servers
* Is stored only so the App works as expected

You can delete the log at any time with Clear on the Logs tab.

4. DATA RETENTION AND DELETION
   Since the developer does not collect or store personal data externally:

* No personal data is retained by the developer
* All app-related data exists only locally on your device

You can delete all stored data at any time by:

* Clearing the app storage via your device settings, or
* Uninstalling the App

Uninstalling the App will permanently delete all associated data.

5. DEVICE PERMISSIONS
   The App may request access to the following features:

* Motion Sensors (accelerometer, gyroscope):
  Used only on the device to detect movement and trigger alarms.
  Readings are not sent anywhere.

* Audio / Speaker:
  Used to play alarm sounds

* Camera / Flashlight:
  Used only to switch the flashlight on and off when the alarm fires
  (No photos or videos are captured)

* Modify System Settings (screen brightness):
  Used only to dim the screen while the alarm is armed and brighten it
  when it fires

* Internet / Advertising ID:
  Used only by Google AdMob to serve and measure ads

The App does not use your location, contacts, microphone or files.

6. DATA SHARING
   The developer does not share any data with third parties. Google
   processes the advertising and purchase data described in sections 7
   and 8 under its own privacy policy.

7. ADVERTISING
   The free version of the App shows ads from Google AdMob. To serve and
   measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP
   address, general device information and how you interact with ads.
   Google's use of this data is described in:

* How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services:
  https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
* Google Privacy Policy:
  https://policies.google.com/privacy

   If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or
   Switzerland, the App asks for your consent before any ads are
   requested. You can change your choice later under Privacy settings at
   the bottom of the Manual tab. You can also reset or delete your
   advertising ID in your device's Android settings.

8. REMOVE ADS PURCHASE
   Remove ads is an optional one-time purchase handled entirely by
   Google Play. The App never sees your payment details. It only asks
   Google Play whether your account owns the purchase, and keeps a copy
   of that answer on your device so ads stay off when you are offline.
   Google's handling of purchases is covered by the Google Privacy
   Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

9. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES
   The only third-party services in the App are Google AdMob and Google
   Play Billing (Google LLC). The App does not use:

* Analytics tools
* Crash reporting or tracking services
* Any server owned by the developer

10. DATA SECURITY
    Settings and the alarm log stay on your device. Data sent to Google
    is encrypted in transit.

11. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY
    The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly
    collect any personal data from them.

12. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY
    This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Updates will be
    posted with a revised Effective Date.
