PRIVACY POLICY FOR MAHJONG HAVEN: CRITTER RESCUE
Effective Date: 17 October 2026

1. INTRODUCTION
   Mahjong Haven: Critter Rescue ("the App", Android package
   com.jaspercherry.mahjonghaven) is a single-player mahjong solitaire
   puzzle game. Your privacy is important, and this Privacy Policy
   explains how information is handled when you use the App.

2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT
   The developer does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

Specifically:

* No account registration is required
* No names, emails, or contact details are collected
* No location data is collected by the developer
* No analytics or tracking by the developer

The only data that leaves your device goes to Google for ads (Google
AdMob, see section 7).

3. LOCAL DATA (GAME PROGRESS AND SETTINGS)
   The App stores the following locally on your device:

* Game progress, such as levels completed, coins, boosters, rescued
  critters, daily puzzle results and streaks
* Settings, such as sound, vibration and reminder preferences

This data:

* Never leaves your device
* Is not transmitted to any servers
* Is stored only so the App works as expected

4. DATA RETENTION AND DELETION
   Since the developer does not collect or store personal data externally:

* No personal data is retained by the developer
* All app-related data exists only locally on your device

You can delete all stored data at any time by:

* Clearing the app storage via your device settings, or
* Uninstalling the App

Uninstalling the App will permanently delete all associated data,
including your game progress.

5. DEVICE PERMISSIONS
   The App may request access to the following features:

* Notifications (optional):
  Used only for an optional daily reminder that a new Daily Puzzle is
  ready. Reminders are scheduled on your device; no server is involved.
  You can turn them off at any time in the App's Settings.

* Vibration:
  Used for short haptic feedback when you play

* Internet / Advertising ID:
  Used only by Google AdMob to serve and measure ads

The App does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or
files.

6. DATA SHARING
   The developer does not share any data with third parties. Google
   processes the advertising data described in section 7 under its own
   privacy policy.

7. ADVERTISING
   The App is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, including optional
   rewarded videos that you can choose to watch for in-game rewards. To
   serve and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising
   ID, IP address (from which an approximate location may be inferred),
   general device information and how you interact with ads. Google's use
   of this data is described in:

* How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services:
  https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
* Google Privacy Policy:
  https://policies.google.com/privacy

   If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or
   Switzerland, the App asks for your consent before any ads are
   requested. You can change your choice later under Settings > Privacy
   & ad choices in the App. You can also reset or delete your
   advertising ID in your device's Android settings.

8. IN-APP PURCHASES
   The App does not offer any in-app purchases.

9. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES
   The only third-party service in the App is Google AdMob (Google LLC).
   The App does not use:

* Analytics tools
* Crash reporting or tracking services
* Any server owned by the developer

10. DATA SECURITY
    Game progress and settings stay on your device. Data sent to Google
    is encrypted in transit.

11. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY
    The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly
    collect any personal data from them.

12. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY
    This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Updates will be
    posted with a revised Effective Date.
